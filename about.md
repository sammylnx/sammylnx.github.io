## What I've done

For four and a half years I worked in Trust & Safety operations for a large social platform. I started as a process associate and became a senior associate and process developer. The job was to apply written policy to real content at scale: deciding what crossed the line, classifying harms, and spotting adversarial actors who studied the rules to get around them.

Over time my work shifted from making decisions to shaping how they were made. I wrote process guidance, audited quality, and worked with subject-matter experts and quality analysts to close the gap between what a policy said and how it was applied.

## Why AI governance

Moderation is where policy meets messy reality, at volume and under time pressure. Automated classifiers did much of the first pass, and people like me handled what they couldn't. AI governance asks the same questions on a much larger scale. What should a system decide on its own? Where must a human stay in the loop? And how do you check that the rules hold up in practice?

That's the work I want to do, in AI policy, trust and safety, red teaming, and governance, risk and compliance.

## What I bring

- **Policy interpretation.** Turning written rules into consistent decisions, and noticing where the wording breaks down.
- **Adversarial thinking.** Years of watching people probe a system for gaps.
- **Harm classification and quality audits.** Knowing what a good decision looks like, and how to measure it.
- **Working across teams.** Subject-matter experts, quality analysts and operations leads all see a policy differently.
- **A maker's background.** A diploma in multimedia and web technologies, four to five years of freelance web development and video production, and a year of content writing.
